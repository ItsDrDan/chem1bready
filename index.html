    References & Hints

    Moles first

    n = M × V (V in liters)
    n = mass ÷ molar mass
    Divide moles by the balanced coefficient.
    The smaller result identifies the limiting reactant.
    Leftover = initial − consumed.

    Titrations & water

    At stoichiometric equivalence, nacid/coefficient = nbase/coefficient. Equal volumes or equal moles are not always required.

    Strong acid + strong base:
    H₃O⁺(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → 2H₂O(l)
    Weak acid + strong base:
    HA(aq) + OH⁻(aq) → A⁻(aq) + H₂O(l)

    Finish reaction stoichiometry before any equilibrium calculation. A weak-acid titration at equivalence contains its conjugate base, which can react with water; equivalence does not always mean pH 7.

    Dissolution & ions

    CaCl₂(s) → Ca²⁺(aq) + 2Cl⁻(aq). Ion coefficients multiply the salt concentration. Water separates and hydrates ions; it does not turn the salt into neutral atoms.

    Usually aqueous: group 1, NH₄⁺, NO₃⁻ salts.
    Usually solid: CO₃²⁻ and PO₄³⁻ salts, except group 1 and NH₄⁺.
    Cl⁻ salts are usually soluble; Ag⁺, Pb²⁺, Hg₂²⁺ are exceptions. These practice sets use unambiguous examples.

    For sparingly soluble salts, ion amounts follow dissolution coefficients. Given molar solubility s, multiply s by each ion coefficient. Calculating s from Ksp belongs to CHEM 1B.

    Lewis → shape → polarity

    Valence counts: H 1; B 3; C 4; N/P 5; O/S 6; F/Cl 7. Add electrons for negative charge and subtract for positive charge. Place bonds and lone pairs, then check formal charge.

    FC = valence electrons − nonbonding electrons − ½ bonding electrons. Each single/double/triple bond counts as one electron group for VSEPR.

    Lone pairs change molecular shape. Bond dipoles may cancel in symmetric molecules. Hybridization is a model; these exercises use standard main-group sp/sp²/sp³ cases.

    MO theory

    Dr. Dan’s MO Theory Part 2 ↗

    Left AO → center MO ← right AO. Fill neutral atoms first, combine electron counts, then adjust for the molecular charge. Diagram layout follows the instructor’s supplied screenshots.

    Fill low energy orbitals first. Pair only after singly occupying degenerate orbitals (Hund’s rule). Bond order = (bonding electrons − antibonding electrons)/2. Unpaired electrons → paramagnetic.

    For B₂/C₂/N₂: π2p below σ2p. For O₂/F₂/Ne₂: σ2p below π2p. Diagrams omit filled 1s core orbitals, whose bonding and antibonding contributions cancel.
